    Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × chains. The peer multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of the protocol's category's live-token peers, shrunk toward the global median by sample size. A protocol is listed as "records no token" when its DefiLlama record carries no token — that is the record's word, not proof it has none. Data: DefiLlama snapshot, . Nothing here is investment advice.
